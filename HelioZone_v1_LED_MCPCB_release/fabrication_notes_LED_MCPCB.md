# Fabrication Notes - LED MCPCB

- Base material: Aluminum core MCPCB, 1.6 mm total thickness.
- Cu thickness: 2 oz top copper.
- Soldermask: white high-reflectance mask, matte finish.
- Silkscreen: black epoxy, top side only.
- Surface finish: ENIG.
- Fiducials: 3 global fiducials, 1 mm Cu with 2 mm mask opening.
- LED polarity marks required for every footprint family.
- Assembly process: lead-free reflow profile compatible with mixed 3030/3535 LEDs.
- Flatness: <=0.75% across panel after reflow.
- Hi-pot: not required on-board; system-level test only.
